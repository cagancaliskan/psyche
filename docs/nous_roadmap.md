## How to read this map

Every step has three things:

- **What it is** — the brick.
- **Depends on** — what has to be solid underneath before this brick can bear weight.
- **Done means** — the *external* test that lets us call it finished. Not "it runs," not "it looks right to me." A claim with a direction we cannot fake.

Each step carries a status tag:

- `PROVEN` — built and verified against hard properties or an external yardstick.
- `BUILT · UNVALIDATED` — the mechanism runs and is deterministic, but its *meaning* hasn't been earned yet.
- `DESIGNED` — exists as a vision document, not as tested code.
- `HORIZON` — genuinely open research. No agreed definition of "done" exists yet, even in the field.

## PHASE 0 — Foundations `PROVEN`

The durable core. This is the part that's already a real, shippable, defensible artifact regardless of how far the rest goes.

**0a. Substrate store** — a graph of concept *nodes* and weighted *roads*. Roads carry emotional charge, access quality, and use-history; nodes stay neutral. Synaptic-tagging-and-consolidation dynamics: roads progress dirt → gravel → road → highway, strengthen with traversal, decay lazily, and "closed" (traumatic) roads stay *leaky, not inert* — a strong cue can still break through.

**0b. Spreading-activation engine** — deterministic, hop-layered single expansion, leaky-closure breakthrough, per-edge flow recording.
> **CONTESTED (2026-07-25) — read this before citing Phase 0's determinism.** The tester's independent 0b2 suite reproduced a cross-process nondeterminism in this engine that the overlay work did not cause and does not fix (**DEFECT-A**). At **library defaults** — `SubstrateStore()` with a live clock, normalization off — a 5-node graph built by `traverse()` alone (S → M0,M1,M2 → T) produced **two distinct results across 48 fresh processes**: `0.10145973529004966` and `0.10145973529004967`. Root cause: convergent accumulation is a naive sequential float add (`activation[target] = activation.get(target, 0.0) + transmitted`, `src/nous_activation.py:287`) whose term order comes from `roads_from()` iterating a **set of uuid4 hex strings** (`src/nous_store.py:444`). Float addition is not associative, so the accumulated sum is run-dependent whenever **three or more** contributions converge on one node; at in-degree ≤ 2 determinism holds bit-exactly across all six hash seeds and all five interpreters. This is the **same bug class as BUG-0b1, one layer upstream** of the coalition-strength site that `math.fsum` fixed.
> Two things this changes about how determinism is checked here: (i) **pinning `PYTHONHASHSEED` cannot establish it** — uuid4 ids differ per process, so seed-pinning does not pin set-iteration order; the defect varies at a *fixed* seed; (ii) **every pre-existing determinism probe compares `round(x, 9)`**, which structurally cannot see a 1-ULP difference — and 1 ULP is exactly what BUG-0b1 was. The tester's new suite compares bit patterns throughout.
> **Status is NOT silently changed.** Phase 0's own "done means" below is determinism across process runs; DEFECT-A falsifies it on in-degree ≥ 3 graphs at library defaults, so `0b`'s `PROVEN` label is **contested and the call is Cagan's** (options and re-validation costs in `DECISIONS.md` D-011). Recorded here rather than resolved here, per the rule that a status change nobody decided is a status change nobody can audit. `0a` is untouched; `0b1`'s own fix (`math.fsum` at both coalition sites) still holds at its own site.

**0b1. Coalition-strength determinism fix** — `PROVEN` *(`docs/BUG-0b1-coalition-strength-determinism.md`)*
`Coalition.strength` summed member activations with plain `sum()` over a uuid4-keyed dict, so two mathematically identical coalitions could differ by 1 ULP and that difference resolved the competition **before** the documented `min creation_seq` tie-break could run — making the tie-break unreachable in practice. Invisible on CPython 3.12; it flipped the winner 8 times in 30 on 3.11 at a *fixed* hash seed. Fixed with `math.fsum` at both sites (exactly rounded, therefore order-independent by construction).
> **Done means — and this is met:** the project's own adversarial probe (`test_T6b`) returns one distinct winner across repeated fresh processes on every swept interpreter, and the full canonical suite is unchanged with no assertion added, weakened or removed. **Caveat now on the record:** the fix is sound *at its site*, but the hazard class the bug document named as "future" turns out to be **present upstream** — DEFECT-A above feeds this site 1-ULP-unstable inputs on in-degree ≥ 3 graphs, so an order-independent sum of order-dependent terms is still order-dependent overall.

**0b2. Conserved activation — divisive normalization at the transmission site** — `BUILT · UNVALIDATED`, **tester verdict FAIL on SPEC-0b2 §6** *(spec `docs/specs/SPEC-0b2-conserved-activation.md`; suite `tests/test_tester_0b2_adversarial.py`, 104 assertions after its 0c re-adjudication — it reported 83 at the FAIL pass itself)*
A capacity constraint for the spreading engine: a road's transmitted activation is divided by sigma plus the competing conductance out of the same source (Carandini & Heeger's canonical form). It ships as the project's third gated overlay — `normalization_sigma=None`, neutral by default and byte-identical to the previous engine — after `broadcast=False` (1d-ii) and `body_sensor=None` (2c). Sigma is fixed at 1.0 **on principle** (D-008), not fitted. **This is arithmetic, not attention**: no cognitive claim is made or implied, and the fan curve it produces is a mechanism characterization on a synthetic star, not a graded behavioral result.
> **Mechanism done means — NOT met.** The tester verified the overlay independently against two oracles (the actual pre-change engine recovered from git, and an independent reimplementation of the documented algorithm) across five CPython minor versions and six hash seeds, and returned **FAIL**: seven of nine invariants hold, **N-2 and N-5 do not**. Three defects, all shipped as asserted-negatives that pass while the defect is present and go red the moment it is fixed. **DEFECT-A** (N-2) is inherited from 0b — see the 0b block above. **DEFECT-B** (N-5, *introduced by 0b2*): the overlay reads each road **twice** per expansion, and lazy decay is applied as of `clock.now()` at each read, so on any clock that advances — the library default — a **lone** out-road has `competing ≈ 1.7e-9` instead of exactly 0 and is attenuated by a factor of `0.99999999827341` rather than exactly 1.0, contradicting SPEC-0b2 §2 and §6 N-5. A caller who follows the spec's own wording gets NaN, with no exception.
> **What holds, verified adversarially:** neutral byte-identity under attack (N-1, on a frozen clock: 17,017 comparisons against the recovered pre-change engine and 15,554 against an independent reimplementation, zero mismatches, including loop-closed on a substrate the pipeline itself built); purity (N-3); order-independence of the conductance sum with a *proven non-vacuous* sensitivity control (N-4); monotonicity (N-6); closed roads competing, including a closed road below the breakthrough threshold that transmits nothing yet still consumes budget (N-8); and no silent silencing (N-9).
> **The blast radius is bounded, and it bounds the next brick too.** The overlay is **not plumbed past `spread_activation`** — `build_activation_field()` and `think()` have no `normalization_sigma` parameter — so no downstream consumer can enable it, DEFECT-B and DEFECT-C reach only direct `spread_activation` callers, and **the §5 default flip is not a one-line change**: it needs plumbing through the field builder, which is a re-validation surface for 1a / 1b / 1c / 1d. **0c. Deterministic road ordering — the repair brick** — `DESIGNED` *(spec `docs/specs/SPEC-0c-deterministic-road-ordering.md`; decisions D-012, D-013)*

> **What it is.** The brick that repairs all three defects the 0b2 tester pass returned, and the route back to `PROVEN` for 0a and 0b. Three changes. **(i) DEFECT-A, fixed at the store and at the arithmetic:** `_out`/`_in` become insertion-ordered so `roads_from()` yields in road-creation order — program order, identical in every process — **and** the convergence accumulator uses `math.fsum`. Both, together, by Cagan's decision (D-013). Ordering alone would give *determinism* (same program, different process → same bits); only `fsum` also gives *order-independence* (a **different** iteration order → same bits). **(ii) DEFECT-B:** materialise each expanding node's roads once, so `out_total` and `road.strength` come from a single `clock.now()` and a lone out-road's `competing` is exactly 0. This also deletes the duplicated-read cost SPEC-0b2 §8 item 4 flagged. **(iii) DEFECT-C:** validate `normalization_sigma` at the boundary (reject non-finite and non-positive), plus a general guard so a non-finite `transmitted` can never pass the floor test — NaN passes `<` silently because every NaN comparison is False.

> **Why `fsum` and not ordering alone — the decisive ground.** Under ordering alone, two of the six asserted-negatives in the tester's 0b2 suite keep passing **truthfully**, because the engine would still be order-dependent, merely pinned. *A fix after which our own adversarial suite can still honestly assert the pathology is a fix at the symptom, not at the cause.* Reinforcing it: ordering-alone's safety depends on a recorded condition being honoured later, and **this repository has already lost that exact bet on this exact hazard** — `experiments/harness.py:290` documented the naive-sum-over-uuid-order hazard in writing, worked around it locally, and the substrate carried `PROVEN` for a month while the engine went unfixed. `nous_activation.py` has meanwhile already written down a future within-layer top-K prune, which is precisely the reordering change that would re-break ordering-alone.

> **A finding that came out of the same decision.** CPython 3.12 changed the builtin `sum()` to **Neumaier compensated summation** for floats. Reproduced across all five interpreters on this machine: `sum() == math.fsum()` is **False** on 3.10/3.11 and **True** on 3.12/3.13/3.14. Since BUG-0b1's fix was exactly `sum(...)` → `math.fsum(...)`, this is the **confirmed mechanism** behind BUG-0b1 being "invisible on 3.12 and reproducible on 3.11" — a difference that bug report recorded as unexplained. The pre-fix code was *accidentally correct on new interpreters*. It also means float correctness in this project is currently a function of which interpreter runs it, wherever a naive `sum()` over floats survives — and **three do** (SPEC-0c §2.5, an open scope call): `nous_workspace.py:428` (the pruned winner's strength, on the 1d-i winner path — the site BUG-0b1's "both sites" missed), `nous_workspace.py:235`, and `nous_store.py:161` (`Road.valence`, feeding a categorical closure decision).

> **The methodology change, which outlives the defects.** Every determinism probe in `tests/` compares `round(x, 9)` and is therefore blind to 1 ULP — which is exactly what BUG-0b1 was. SPEC-0c §5.1 enumerates **18 such call sites across 13 files**, split builder-owned (8) and tester-owned (5); all must compare full bit patterns. And seed-pinning is demoted from *the guarantee* to a regression probe: iteration order derives from uuid4 values, which differ per process regardless of `PYTHONHASHSEED`, so the swept axis never controlled the mechanism. What replaces it is direct order-perturbation and uuid4-invariance probing. The generalisation, for the paper: **"A coverage axis is only evidence for the property it can perturb."**

> **Re-validation cost, stated up front.** Every brick that consumes `roads_from` must be re-verified: 0b, 1a, 1b, 1c, 1d-i, 1d-ii, 2c. Only order-dependent float accumulations move, and they move by ULPs — which BUG-0b1 established is enough to flip a winner 8 times in 30. **1e and 1e-prime need no re-run:** both NO-GOs are structural, not numerical, and a ULP cannot revive a stratum that was tied by construction. All of this is **mechanism re-verification, not meaning re-validation** — no cognitive claim is at stake, and the meaning bar does not move.

> **Why the fan effect ESCAPES 1e's wall (the optimistic read, which is real).** The fan effect rides on the **NUMBER of a source's out-edges (its out-degree / fan)** — a **distinct, separable axis** from the **weight of any one edge**. In 1e the prediction variable (SWOW strength) *was* the edge weight; here fan and edge weight are different graph properties, so one can hold edge strength and distance fixed while varying only fan. The fan effect therefore **does avoid 1e's strength=weight coupling** — on that axis it is the cleaner test.
> **Why it is NO-GO anyway (the harder wall — the generating mechanism is absent).** Anderson's fan effect is, mechanistically, a **shared-budget / normalization** effect: ACT-R explains it by a source emitting a *conserved* activation that is **divided among its associations** (`S_ji ≈ S − ln(fan)`), so more fan → smaller share per target → weaker retrieval. **This engine has no such conservation.** In `spread_activation` (`src/nous_activation.py`) each out-road receives the source's **full** activation — `outgoing = node_act`, then `transmitted = outgoing × road.strength × decay_per_hop` — with **no `/ len(out_roads)`**, no per-source budget, no division across out-edges. The empirical confirmation is exact and verbatim: a source's out-degree 1 vs 8 gives the target the **byte-identical** activation, and the full sweep is **flat to the last digit**:
> ```
> F   mean target activation      spread (max-min)
> 1   0.2433743975                0.0
> 2   0.2433743975                0.0
> 4   0.2433743975                0.0
> 8   0.2433743975                0.0
> 16  0.2433743975                0.0
> VERDICT: DOES NOT MATCH: activation is flat across fan — the engine does not normalize by out-degree.
> ```

The closed loop inherits the bare engine's flat fan response and adds none of its own.

## Rules of the road (the discipline that makes lego bricks become a car)

These are the habits already paid for in Phase 0. They're what keep the stack compounding instead of collapsing three layers up.

1. **Reinvent the idea, borrow the scaffolding.** The novelty is the architecture, not the syntax or the substrate language. Spend the reinvention budget only where the genuinely new thing lives.
2. **Every brick proves itself before the next snaps on.** Loop-closure. A brick that "passes on a friendly input" hasn't passed — it's just waiting to fail under load.
3. **External yardsticks, never self-authored inputs.** The fastest way to fool ourselves is to grade the system on data we wrote. Relatedness, norms, baselines: all from outside.
4. **Keep `PROVEN` and `DESIGNED` in separate boxes, always.** The vision docs are description, not specification. Calling something built when it's only designed is the one move that poisons trust in everything else.
5. **Every phase ends in something shippable.** Even if the road stops at any phase, what's behind us should stand on its own as a real, citable artifact. Phase 0 already does.

## Where we go from here

1c has done its job for now: its mechanism is verified, and its meaning experiment ran and returned an honest **NOT YET** (decisively better than control, short of the absolute ARI bar — under-merging vs a coarse standard partition). Crucially, a decision logged 2026-06-20 establishes that 1c §6.2 was never the right *headline* for "meaning" anyway — comparing one layer's partition to an automatic partition of the same graph is low-value evidence of cognition. The project's **primary meaning bar moves to a behavioral effect** (meaning is what the system *does*) — first attempted as semantic priming at 1e (NO-GO), then re-routed to the fan effect (also NO-GO), and now, with the association family exhausted, **re-routed to interoceptive modulation (2c), graded against real machine telemetry** (below). 1c's §6.2 remains a recorded secondary/structural check, and 1c is **not** reopened to chase the absolute ARI number (the granularity tradeoffs are recorded as **dormant, conditional** items — revisit only if a later step shows grouping grain is load-bearing, and only on fresh data, never tuned to the EXP-1c set).

Since then, **1d-i (competition + over-swallow pruning) AND 1d-ii (broadcast + bounded feedback) have both been built and their mechanism is tester-VERIFIED (PASS)**. 1d-i flips the over-swallow asserted-negative inherited from 1c through the live loop, via a parameter-free cohesion-valley / relative-attachment watershed accepted (2026-06-20) as the intended design (the divergence from literal membership is safe-direction only, with one corner case logged as an accepted known-limitation). 1d-ii adds the single bounded broadcast write that 1d-i's verified selection feeds — proven bounded both ways (no divergence: plateaus, ≤ 1.0, boundedness structural from saturation, no clamp; no non-vacuous lock-in: escape after 80 cycles on a bridged graph, with a freeze probe). 1d makes **no** cognitive claim: closing an information loop with bounded feedback is a mechanism result, not a cognitive or consciousness one. **The cognitive cycle now closes end-to-end**, and because no external-data claim is made, **1d overall sits at `BUILT · UNVALIDATED`**.

The honest next action *was* **1e (semantic priming)** — and it was carried all the way to a blind, pre-registered stimulus selection, where it returned a frozen **NO-GO**. The three-arm conjunctive design was fully specified (all five SPEC-1e decisions resolved, pre-registration frozen with an anti-tamper notice), and the §10 selection module was run **blind** on the full SWOW graph (topology + R123.Strength only, no activation). It revealed the design is **infeasible on SWOW for anti-circularity reasons**: the §6.2 loop-is-doing-real-work gate — which exists precisely so an arm-(b) "pass" cannot be the SWOW-strength→edge-weight map read straight back out — **cannot be populated.** Because the coarse saturating weight ladder (`W = min(10, 1+floor(strength·100))`) makes a prime's HIGH band almost always outweigh its LOW band, the weight-blind leverage stratum collapses to **N_S0 = 7 < 30**, and the pre-registered fallback (`V = r_MA − r_W ≥ 0.20`) is **arithmetically unreachable** (S+ dominance gives `r_W ≈ 0.97`, leaving no 0.20 of value-added headroom under the rank-biserial cap of 1.0). This was learned **before any graded number** — no `think()`, no activation, no Δ — so it is a **structural finding, not a failure**: the discipline refused to manufacture an uninterpretable "pass." The honest statement: *a spreading-activation substrate whose edge weights encode SWOW strength cannot, on this corpus, with both the edge-weight and target-popularity mechanical paths controlled, separate genuine priming-through-the-loop from its own weight map* — which does **not** claim priming is absent, only that weight and strength are too coupled here to tell them apart. Full record: SPEC-1e §14, EXP-1e §15, and the verbatim selection stdout `experiments/results/priming_1e_selection_output.txt`. **No mechanism status changed: 1c and 1d stay `BUILT · UNVALIDATED`; Phase 0 stays `PROVEN`; no lower brick reopened; no threshold loosened.**

The behavioral primary meaning bar was then re-routed to the **fan effect** — the pre-named, lower-circularity backup — and a gating feasibility pre-check (`docs/specs/SPEC-fan-effect.md`) found **that NO-GO too, for a harder reason than 1e.** The fan effect genuinely **escapes** 1e's strength=weight coupling — it rides on **out-degree (fan)**, a distinct axis from edge weight — **but** the engine has **no shared-budget / out-degree normalization** that the fan effect's mechanism requires (ACT-R divides a conserved source activation across associations, `S_ji ≈ S − ln(fan)`; this engine instead feeds **every** out-edge the source's **full** activation — `spread_activation` in `src/nous_activation.py` has no `/ len(out_roads)`). The Phase 2A characterization already measured the consequence: the fan sweep is **flat to the last digit** (target activation `0.2433743975`, identical across fan ∈ {1,2,4,8,16}; verbatim in `experiments/results/fan_effect_output.txt`), and the closed loop inherits it (1d-i competition normalizes by **coalition size**, 1d-ii broadcast has **no out-degree term**). So this is **a completed empirical result, not a pending one** — re-running would re-measure flat. Full record: `docs/specs/SPEC-fan-effect.md`.

This yields the **META-FINDING of this freeze: both association-family meaning bars dead-end for one root reason — every meaning claim drawn from spreading activation over the SWOW-weighted graph is either (a) a readback of the weights the graph was built from (priming, 1e), or (b) an effect the engine structurally cannot produce (fan).** The behavioral **primary meaning bar is therefore re-routed to interoception (2c)** — its signal is **real machine telemetry**, external to the substrate and to SWOW, so it is immune to both walls and gradable against the telemetry itself with **no human-norm circularity.** 2c is the strongest original idea on the map and was already sanctioned to pull early; the two NO-GOs make it the clear next move. It still gets a **feasibility pre-check first** (the 1e/1e′ lesson: confirm the bar can be populated and graded *before* a graded run) — it is the escape route, not a guaranteed result. **(Since superseded — the 2c feasibility pre-check has cleared and its mechanism is BUILT · tester-VERIFIED, meaning still UNVALIDATED; see the 2c block.)** Separately, the **engine-change path** (adding source-activation / out-degree normalization, the only way a fan effect could exist) is held as a **later, explicitly-framed architecture decision, NOT a meaning-bar rescue:** it would change the meaning of every prior activation result (forcing re-validation of 1c/1d), and adopting it *in order to* manufacture the effect would not earn it. **No mechanism status changed by this freeze: 1c and 1d stay `BUILT · UNVALIDATED`; Phase 0 stays `PROVEN`; no lower brick reopened; no threshold loosened.** Both NO-GOs are the **anti-circularity discipline working** — catching unwinnable designs before any graded run — not project failure.
