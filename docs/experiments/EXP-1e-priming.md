## 1. Claim under test (narrow, exact — verbatim from SPEC-1e §1)

> Through the closed cognitive loop, a prime activates **strongly-associated** targets **more** than it
> activates **matched weakly-/un-associated** targets — where "associated" is defined by **external human
> SWOW association norms**, never by us — and this difference **survives, conjunctively, a control that
> holds graph reachability constant (arm a) AND a control that holds path distance constant while varying
> only association strength (arm b).** The claim is about the **DIRECTION** of the effect (related >
> unrelated), graded against human data.

This is a claim about a **mechanistic activation-magnitude difference in a pre-stated direction**, measured
**through the closed loop** via the **M-A broadcast-trace Δ** metric (the genuinely closed-loop measurement
that distinguishes 1e from the bare-engine 2B study), with **M-B bare-spread as corroboration and the bridge
to 2B.** It earns the word "cognitive" only in the limited sense that the *direction* matches a robust human
behavioral phenomenon (automatic semantic priming) measured on data the project did not author.

## 1.1 What 1e is NOT — the honest framing (verbatim from SPEC-1e §1.1; non-negotiable, travels with every report)

- 1e is **NOT** a claim of **understanding**, **meaning in the human sense**, **comprehension**, **concepts**,
  **consciousness**, **awareness**, or that the system **"knows what words mean."** A directional activation
  difference is **not** comprehension and may never be described as such.
- The system has **no semantics.** "DOCTOR is more active after NURSE" is a statement about **activation
  spreading over a graph whose edges were built from human association counts** — it is *not* a statement that
  the system grasps doctors, nurses, or any relation between them.
- The mechanism Nous exercises corresponds, at most, to the **automatic / prospective / spreading-activation
  component** of human priming (Neely 1991): fast, association-driven activation. It does **not** model the
  strategic/expectancy or retrospective relatedness-checking components, and it is **not** a model of the
  lexical-decision *task*.
- A **positive** result establishes only: *on external human norms, with the prediction fixed in advance and
  reachability/distance controlled, the system reproduces the direction of automatic semantic priming.* A
  **negative** result is equally honest, reported as-is; it leaves the cognitive claim unearned and does not
  reopen lower bricks.

This is **DESIGNED / a frozen protocol, not a result.** `PROVEN` and `DESIGNED` stay in separate boxes
(CLAUDE.md rule 3). Any overclaim word above appears **only inside an explicit negation.**

## 2. The human direction this must reproduce

In humans, a target is recognized faster/more strongly when preceded by a **strongly associated** prime than
by an **unrelated** (or weakly associated) prime, at matched conditions (Meyer & Schvaneveldt 1971; Neely
1991; the automatic/spreading-activation component, Collins & Loftus 1975). The direction is fixed in advance:
**related/strong > unrelated/weak.** The system must move activation (and the broadcast-trace Δ) **up** for the
strong/related arm relative to the matched control — never down. A reversal (weak/unrelated more active)
falsifies the relevant arm.

## 4. The metric (SPEC-1e §4, RESOLVED: M-A headline, M-B corroboration, M-C rejected)

### M-A — Broadcast-trace Δ (HEADLINE; the genuine closed-loop metric)

Measured through `think()` with the 1d-ii broadcast, by the builder's §9 instrument (B-i):

1. Build the frozen SWOW substrate once (`Clock(simulated=True)`, library default activation knobs).
2. **Prime cycle:** `think(store, prime_utterance, broadcast=True)` — perception → 1c cluster → field →
   coalitions → 1d-i winner → 1d-ii broadcast strengthens the winner's internal roads (the bounded
   conductance trace).
3. **Probe:** read the **target's activation under a spread from the prime** on the **broadcast-modified**
   store vs a **pristine copy** (no broadcast). The scalar is **Δ = activation_after_broadcast −
   activation_before_broadcast**, per (prime, target).
4. Aggregate by arm/condition; test by a **within-prime sign test** on Δ (categorical arms) and Spearman
   (graded arm).

- **Why headline:** it is the **only** metric that exercises 1d-ii's broadcast — the thing that makes 1e a
  closed-loop result and not a re-run of 2B. The claim becomes "*the prime's broadcast trace preferentially
  eases its strongly-associated targets.*"
- **Named cost (reported, not hidden):** the broadcast strengthens only roads **internal to the winning
  coalition.** A target outside the winner gets Δ = 0 regardless of association strength. So M-A measures
  priming **within the broadcast's reach**, and the validator **must report**, per arm, the fraction of
  strong-related vs weak/unrelated targets that fall inside the winner (a structural property, and a possible
  reason for a null — reported, not hidden).

### M-B — Bare-spread (CORROBORATION; the 2B metric, reused; the bridge to 2B — NOT the closed-loop result)

Single spread from the prime, read target activation (no `think()`, no broadcast) — exactly 2B's
`harness.read_targets`. Used to **replicate 2B under the new three-arm controls**, so the controlled result is
directly comparable to the prior partial one. It does **not** use the broadcast, so it is **not** "through the
closed loop"; it is reported as mechanism-level corroboration. **The headline closed-loop claim rides on M-A.**

### M-C — Selection/winner metric (REJECTED, recorded for the audit trail)

Whether the strong target *wins the workspace* more often. Rejected: dominated by which sources the current
percept lights up (1d-i seeds from perceived sources), so it mostly measures perception/clustering, not
priming; and it is a coarse binary discarding the graded signal.

### Normalization

**Raw Δ is primary.** A measurement-layer **normalized** readout (Δ as a share of the prime's outgoing total,
via `read_targets`' `outgoing_total`) is reported as **secondary, clearly labeled the same value rescaled per
prime** (per the proven fan-effect finding the engine does not normalize by out-degree, so the normalized
readout is not an independent competition measure). All activation knobs at library defaults, **held identical
across all conditions and arms, and asserted as a control.** Each of the three arms is measured under **both**
M-A (headline) and M-B (corroboration); the conjunctive verdict is read on **M-A**, with M-B alongside.

## 5. The three-arm control structure (SPEC-1e §5/§3.2, RESOLVED: D-1 + arm b, conjunctive)

The crux (SPEC-1e §3): **if a positive priming result could be fully explained by "a path exists in the graph"
(reachability) or "a shorter path decays less" (distance/decay), it is trivial topology, not priming.** 2B
flagged **two** holes — reachability (its UNRELATED arm was pinned at exactly 0 for all 300 pairs, i.e.
out-of-horizon) **and** mechanical decay (DIRECT > MEDIATED is partly 1-hop-vs-2-hop attenuation). Each hole
gets its own categorical control arm; the third arm is graded corroboration.

- **Arm (a) — reachability control** *(closes 2B hole a)*. STRONG-RELATED vs UNRELATED-BUT-REACHABLE at
  **matched distance** — **both in horizon, both non-zero in raw activation.** Predicted direction:
  STRONG-RELATED activation **and** Δ **>** MATCHED-UNRELATED-BUT-REACHABLE. *Rules out: "it lit up only
  because a path exists / the control got nothing because it was beyond the horizon."*
- **Arm (b) — distance/decay control** *(closes 2B hole b; the NEW arm; co-headline)*. HIGH-strength vs
  LOW-strength SWOW targets held at **EQUAL graph path-distance**, in a **categorical** HIGH-vs-LOW contrast
  with a **within-prime sign test** and fixed direction. Primary realization: **both direct (1 hop)**; optional
  distance-2 sub-arm by **bottleneck path-strength** (reported separately, dropped if it cannot populate).
  Predicted direction: at equal distance, HIGH activation **and** Δ **>** LOW. *Rules out: "DIRECT beat
  MEDIATED only because 1 hop decays less than 2."*
- **Arm (c) — graded corroboration** *(supporting; not a categorical pass/fail)*. Continuous SWOW-strength →
  activation **Spearman slope** among **equidistant direct (1-hop)** targets. Predicted: **Spearman ρ > 0.**

### 5.1 THE BINDING CONJUNCTIVE VERDICT RULE (pre-registered; SPEC-1e §5/§7.2)

> **The headline genuine-priming claim is EARNED if and only if the pre-stated direction holds at the
> pre-stated effect size on BOTH arm (a) AND arm (b)** (each measured on the M-A broadcast-Δ headline metric),
> **with arm (c) in the same direction as corroboration, and determinism intact.**

- **Passing only arm (c)** (graded slope) while **failing arm (b)** → honest **NOT YET**, reported explicitly
  as **"the effect is explained by topology/decay, not association."**
- **Failing arm (a)** (no separation once reachability/distance held constant) → honest **NOT YET** (the
  reachability hole is open; the 2B effect was reachability, not priming).
- **Arm (c) can NEVER rescue a failed categorical arm.** It only corroborates a passing one.
- **Why both, not one:** arm (a) makes one compared target *unrelated* but cannot, alone, rule out that the
  related target sat at a shorter effective distance; arm (b) holds distance *exactly* equal but cannot, alone,
  rule out that the whole effect lives beyond the horizon (both its targets are in-horizon by construction).
  Only the **conjunction** closes both of 2B's holes at once. Neither single arm is sufficient.

## 6. THE MAKE-OR-BREAK — the §3.2-line-87 residual, and the loop-is-doing-real-work check

This section is the crux of whether 1e measures anything real, and is **part of the frozen pre-registration.**

### 6.1 The residual confound, named (SPEC-1e §3.2 line 87)

Because the substrate **encodes SWOW strength AS the edge weight** (the fixed `traverse_count =
min(10, 1 + floor(R123.Strength · 100))` map in `swow.traverse_count_for_strength`), arm (b)'s "HIGH beats
LOW at equal distance" is **partly mechanical by construction** — a naive pass could simply be the
strength→weight map read straight back out at the target. This is a **circularity cousin**: the answer
baked into the setup. SPEC-1e §3.2 (line 87) flagged this honestly rather than hiding it. This
pre-registration now **defends against it concretely**, below, not merely by acknowledgment.

A discreteness fact about this specific map, fixed here so the residual is fully understood: with median
R123.Strength = 0.01, strengths ≤ 0.01 all map to **traverse_count = 1** (the floor of the map), and the map
is **capped at 10**. So the strength→weight signal is a **coarse, saturating, integer** ladder, not a smooth
readback — which is one reason (but not a sufficient one) the loop's continuous dynamics could either add real
information or merely pass the coarse ladder through. The check below decides which, by measurement.

### 6.2 The pre-registered LOOP-IS-DOING-REAL-WORK check (HIGH/LOW value-added-over-bare-weight statistic + frozen margin)

**Purpose:** quantify whether the **closed loop** (1c clustering + 1d-i competition/pruning + 1d-ii broadcast)
adds **genuine HIGH-vs-LOW separation over and above** what the raw incoming-edge-weight map already produces —
**measured on the exact same HIGH/LOW contrast that arm (b)'s verdict rides on**, not on a different
population. If the loop's HIGH-vs-LOW separation does not exceed the bare-weight map's own HIGH-vs-LOW
prediction by the frozen margin, then an arm-(b) "pass" is the strength→weight ladder read straight back out at
the target — a **pass-through of edge weights, not priming** — and is declared **MEANINGLESS** (an honest NOT
YET, *"mechanical weight map, not priming-through-the-loop"*).

**Why this replaces the earlier all-targets divergence statistic (the population-mismatch fix, pre-data —
2026-06-21; see Revision Note at the foot of this file).** The original §6.2 gate read **D̄ = median over ALL
of a prime's direct targets of (1 − Spearman(raw-weight ranking, M-A Δ ranking))**. But arm (b)'s verdict is
adjudicated **only** on the **HIGH-vs-LOW extreme bands.** Those are different populations: the loop could clear
an all-targets divergence bar by reordering **mid-range** targets while the **HIGH-vs-LOW gap arm (b) actually
rides on** passes through as a coarse-ladder mechanical readback. A guard measured on a different contrast than
the thing it guards does not protect the axis it exists to protect. The binding gate below is therefore
re-pinned to the **HIGH/LOW contrast itself**, and **D̄ is retained only as a reported secondary diagnostic**
(§6.2.1).

**The no-loop-dynamics baseline (the barest readback).** For each prime P and each of P's direct (1-hop)
targets T, the **raw incoming-edge-weight** is the integer traverse-count weight of the single P→T edge,
`W(P,T) = traverse_count_for_strength(R123.Strength(P→T))` (equivalently, the stored road strength of P→T
before any broadcast). This baseline involves **no competition, no broadcast, no pruning, no multi-hop spread**
— it is the rawest possible "stronger SWOW edge → ranked higher" ordering, strictly barer than M-B bare-spread
(which already includes a full spread) and far barer than M-A. Per prime, this baseline induces a ranking of
P's direct targets by `W(P,T)`.

**The loop-side ordering.** For the **same** prime P and the **same** set of P's direct targets, the
**M-A broadcast-Δ** value (the headline closed-loop measurement; §4) induces a second ranking of those targets.
(The corroborating M-B ranking is also computed and reported, but the check's verdict is read on M-A, because
M-A is the metric the headline rides on and the metric that includes the broadcast — the loop's only write.)

**The bare-weight HIGH/LOW baseline separation (FIXED here — the no-loop prediction on the SAME contrast).**
For each arm-(b) distance-1 prime P with its HIGH-band target T_H and LOW-band target T_L (§9.2), the
no-loop-dynamics baseline predicts the HIGH−LOW separation directly from the integer weight map:
**`W_gap(P) = W(P,T_H) − W(P,T_L)`**, where `W(P,T) = traverse_count_for_strength(R123.Strength(P→T))` (the
bare-weight map above; **no competition, no broadcast, no pruning, no spread**). By the band construction
`W_gap(P) ≥ 0` (HIGH is the per-prime top decile, LOW the per-prime 10th–25th percentile). Partition the arm-(b)
distance-1 primes into two strata by their **bare-weight** prediction:

- **Stratum S0 — the WEIGHT-BLIND stratum (the leverage stratum):** `W_gap(P) = 0`. The saturating integer
  ladder (§6.1: median R123.Strength = 0.01 collapses all strengths ≤ 0.01 to `traverse_count = 1`, and the map
  caps at 10) maps P's HIGH-band and LOW-band targets to the **same integer weight.** The bare-weight baseline
  therefore predicts **exactly zero** HIGH−LOW separation for these primes — its within-prime sign-test
  proportion is the tie/chance rate (0.50) and its HIGH−LOW separation is identically 0, **by construction.**
- **Stratum S+ — the WEIGHT-INFORMATIVE stratum:** `W_gap(P) > 0`. Here the bare-weight map already predicts
  HIGH > LOW; direction on S+ is **free to the baseline** and cannot, alone, evidence the loop.

The achieved sizes `N_S0` and `N_S+` are **reported, never assumed.**

**Why the comparison is apples-to-apples (the common-currency resolution).** Bare weights are coarse integers
(1–10) and M-A Δ is in activation units, so "M-A exceeds the weight baseline by a margin" is ill-defined if one
tries to subtract an integer gap from an activation gap. The resolution is **stratum S0**, where the bare-weight
baseline's predicted separation is **identically zero** — so the comparison reduces to a well-defined,
scale-free question: *does the closed loop produce a HIGH>LOW separation precisely where the weight map predicts
none?* No integer-vs-activation rescaling is needed; the baseline value being exceeded is a fixed **0.** And
because the HIGH/LOW bands are **defined by weight**, the bare map already "separates" HIGH>LOW in the right
direction on S+ for free — so value-added **cannot** be a direction/ordering claim (the baseline gets ordering
for free wherever weights differ); it must be the **magnitude/reliability/structure** of separation **where the
coarse weight prediction is null.** S0 is exactly that locus.

**The binding value-added statistic (FIXED here) — measured on the HIGH/LOW contrast, value over bare weight:**

> **PRIMARY (S0 powered, `N_S0 ≥ 30`):** the closed loop must produce a HIGH > LOW M-A separation **on the
> weight-blind stratum S0** at the **same effect size arm (b) itself requires (§7)**:
> **(L-S0-sign)** within-prime sign test on **M-A Δ** over S0: HIGH > LOW in **≥ 65%** of `N_S0` primes, with
> two-sided sign-test **p < 0.05** in the predicted direction; **AND**
> **(L-S0-median)** median(HIGH M-A Δ − LOW M-A Δ) over S0 **≥ 0.01** (raw Δ units).
> Because the bare-weight baseline's HIGH−LOW separation on S0 is **0 by construction** (both bands tie at the
> same integer weight), clearing (L-S0-sign) ∧ (L-S0-median) is, by definition, the loop **adding** HIGH/LOW
> separation **the weight map cannot produce** — value-added over bare weight, on the same contrast arm (b)
> rides on. **The pre-registered margin is exactly this: M-A HIGH>LOW on S0 must exceed the baseline's null
> (proportion 0.50, separation 0.0) by the §7 arm-(b) effect size — a separation of ≥ 0.01 Δ-units at ≥ 65% in
> direction where the weight map predicts 0.0 at 50%.**
>
> **FALLBACK (S0 underpowered, `N_S0 < 30`):** if too few primes land in the weight-blind stratum to power the
> S0 sign test, the value-added is instead measured **over ALL arm-(b) distance-1 primes by partialling out the
> bare-weight gap.** Define, on a common prime set, the **M-A HIGH−LOW rank-biserial** `r_MA` (the matched-pairs
> rank-biserial correlation = `2·(fraction of primes with HIGH M-A Δ > LOW M-A Δ) − 1`, ∈ [−1, +1]) and the
> **bare-weight HIGH−LOW rank-biserial** `r_W` (identically computed on `sign(W_gap(P))`: `+1` per S+ prime,
> `0` per S0 prime; = `2·(N_S+ / N_arm(b)) − 1` since `W_gap ≥ 0`). Both are on the **same scale-free [−1, +1]
> rank-biserial axis** (the common currency). The **frozen value-added margin** is:
> **`V = r_MA − r_W ≥ 0.20`** — the loop's HIGH>LOW separation, expressed as a rank-biserial, must exceed the
> bare-weight map's own HIGH>LOW separation, expressed identically, by **at least 0.20** on the rank-biserial
> scale. (This keeps the original 0.20 magnitude, but now on the HIGH/LOW contrast and net of what the weight
> map already delivers, instead of an all-targets divergence.)

**The FROZEN gate (pre-registered, not post-hoc):**

> The loop is declared **doing real work on the arm-(b) contrast** iff the applicable form above holds —
> **(L-S0-sign) ∧ (L-S0-median)** when `N_S0 ≥ 30`, else **`V = r_MA − r_W ≥ 0.20`.** If it does **not** hold,
> the loop is declared to add **no** HIGH/LOW separation beyond the bare-weight map, and **any arm-(b) "pass" is
> declared MEANINGLESS** — the strength→weight ladder read straight back out at the target, not
> priming-through-the-loop — and reported as an honest **NOT YET**, *"mechanical weight map, not
> priming-through-the-loop."* Which form was applied, and `N_S0` / `N_S+`, are **reported.**

**Justification of the margin (fixed in advance, as the meaning expert).** The bands are *defined* by weight,
so the bare-weight map predicts HIGH>LOW for free wherever the integers differ (S+) and predicts **nothing** —
a tie — wherever the saturating ladder collapses them (S0). The honest locus of loop value is therefore S0,
where any HIGH>LOW the loop produces is information the coarse weight prediction does **not** contain. Requiring
the loop to clear, **on S0**, the *same* effect size arm (b) must clear overall is the natural "value-added"
bar: it is neither a free pass (S0's baseline is a hard 0.0/50%, so the loop must genuinely separate the bands
through competition + pruning + broadcast where weights are tied) nor a perverse over-ask (it is not demanding
the loop *invert* or *massively reorder* strength — the intended direction is that strength predicts
activation). The fallback's `V ≥ 0.20` preserves the original, already-justified 0.20 magnitude, re-pinned to
the HIGH/LOW contrast and net of the weight map's own contribution, so the bar's spirit is unchanged when S0 is
too thin to stand alone. The strata definition (`W_gap` cut at 0), the S0 powering floor (`N_S0 ≥ 30`), the S0
effect-size bar (≥ 65% at p < 0.05 and ≥ 0.01 Δ-units), and the fallback margin (`V ≥ 0.20`) are **frozen here
and not loosened after seeing data.**

#### 6.2.1 D̄ retained as a REPORTED SECONDARY DIAGNOSTIC (no longer the binding gate)

The original all-targets divergence statistic is **kept and reported as a secondary diagnostic** (it still
characterizes how much the loop reorders the *global* per-prime ranking, which is informative), but it is **no
longer the binding `(b-loop-does-work)` gate** — the population mismatch above is why. For the record, the
secondary diagnostic is: per prime P with ≥ 3 direct targets carrying ≥ 2 distinct raw-weight values, the
Spearman rank correlation `ρ_P` between the raw-weight ranking `W(P,·)` and the M-A broadcast-Δ ranking over P's
direct targets; `D_P = 1 − ρ_P ∈ [0, 2]`; **D̄ = median D_P** across qualifying primes (primes with < 3 direct
targets or no raw-weight variation excluded; excluded count reported). **D̄ ≥ 0.20** is reported as a
**descriptive corroborating signal** that the loop reorders the global ranking, but it **does not** by itself
gate arm (b), and a high D̄ driven by mid-range reordering **cannot** substitute for the HIGH/LOW value-added
gate above.

### 6.3 The MEANINGFUL result is the conjunction (frozen statement)

The genuine arm-(b) priming result is the **conjunction of two pre-registered facts:**

> **(i) the loop demonstrably adds HIGH/LOW value over the bare-weight map** — the §6.2 value-added gate holds
> (HIGH > LOW separation on the weight-blind stratum S0 at the §7 arm-(b) effect size when `N_S0 ≥ 30`, else
> `V = r_MA − r_W ≥ 0.20`), i.e. the closed loop separates HIGH from LOW **where, or beyond what, the raw
> edge-weight map predicts** — **AND (ii) arm (b) HIGH > LOW still holds** at its §7 effect size on the full
> arm-(b) population **through that work.**

**Explicit, frozen:** if arm (b) passes **only** because of the mechanical strength→weight construction — i.e.
the loop adds **no** HIGH/LOW separation over the bare-weight map by the §6.2 value-added gate (fails on S0, or
`V < 0.20` in the fallback) — that is **NOT a genuine priming result and must be reported as such:** an honest
**NOT YET**, *"mechanical weight map, not priming-through-the-loop."* A HIGH>LOW separation that the bare-weight
map already produces (no value added on the same contrast) does not earn the headline. (Arm (a) is not subject
to this specific check, because its control target is *unrelated* — there is no strength→weight ladder to read
back between a prime and a node it has no SWOW edge to; arm (a)'s own guard is the distance-equalization report,
§7.)

## 7. Pre-registered predictions and per-arm effect-size thresholds (FIXED — the falsifiable part)

All thresholds below are fixed **now**, before any graded number, by the validator as the meaning expert.
They are **not** loosened after seeing data. Each categorical arm is judged on the **M-A broadcast-Δ headline
metric**; M-B is reported alongside as corroboration and must not *contradict* the M-A direction for a clean
pass (a §7.1 falsifier covers M-A/M-B disagreement).

Notation: per arm, `N_arm` = number of primes contributing a within-prime pair to that arm (reported per arm,
never pooled into one count). The within-prime sign test counts, per prime, whether the strong/HIGH member
exceeds the weak/unrelated/LOW member.

### Arm (a) — reachability control (CATEGORICAL; co-decisive)

- **Direction:** STRONG-RELATED **>** MATCHED-UNRELATED-BUT-REACHABLE, on both raw activation **and** M-A Δ.
- **Effect-size threshold (ALL must hold):**
  - **(a-sign)** within-prime sign test on **M-A Δ**: STRONG-RELATED > MATCHED-CONTROL in **≥ 65%** of
    `N_arm(a)` primes, with two-sided sign-test **p < 0.05** in the predicted direction.
  - **(a-median-Δ)** median(STRONG-RELATED M-A Δ) − median(MATCHED-CONTROL M-A Δ) **≥ 0.01** (raw Δ units).
  - **(a-median-raw)** median(STRONG-RELATED raw activation) − median(MATCHED-CONTROL raw activation)
    **≥ 0.05** (raw activation units; the M-B corroboration must show the same direction).
  - **(a-control-nonzero)** the MATCHED-UNRELATED-BUT-REACHABLE control is **non-zero in raw activation for ≥
    90% of `N_arm(a)` primes** (it must be genuinely in-horizon — if the control is all/near-zero again, the
    reachability confound is NOT broken; see §7.1).
  - **(a-distance-equalized)** the achieved per-arm hop-distance distributions are **equalized**: the median
    hop-distance of the STRONG-RELATED targets and of the MATCHED-CONTROL targets differ by **≤ 1 hop**, and
    this achieved distribution is **reported** (a §7.1 falsifier if not equalized).

### Arm (b) — distance/decay control (CATEGORICAL; co-decisive; subject to the §6 loop-work check)

- **Direction:** at **equal path-distance**, HIGH-strength **>** LOW-strength, on both raw activation **and**
  M-A Δ (distance-1 primary; distance-2 sub-arm reported separately if populated).
- **Effect-size threshold (ALL must hold, on the distance-1 primary realization):**
  - **(b-sign)** within-prime sign test on **M-A Δ**: HIGH > LOW in **≥ 65%** of `N_arm(b)` primes, with
    two-sided sign-test **p < 0.05** in the predicted direction.
  - **(b-median-Δ)** median(HIGH M-A Δ) − median(LOW M-A Δ) **≥ 0.01** (raw Δ units).
  - **(b-median-raw)** median(HIGH raw activation) − median(LOW raw activation) **≥ 0.05** (raw activation
    units; M-B corroboration same direction).
  - **(b-distance-constant)** distance is **genuinely held constant** across the two bands (both targets at
    exactly 1 hop in the primary realization), **and both bands populate** for `N_arm(b)` primes — reported,
    not assumed.
  - **(b-loop-does-work) — THE MAKE-OR-BREAK GATE (HIGH/LOW value-added over bare weight; §6.2):** the closed
    loop must add HIGH>LOW separation **the bare raw-edge-weight map does not already produce, on the same
    HIGH/LOW contrast.** Concretely, the §6.2 value-added gate must hold: **when `N_S0 ≥ 30`,** HIGH > LOW on the
    **weight-blind stratum S0** (`W_gap = W(P,T_H) − W(P,T_L) = 0`, where the bare-weight baseline predicts
    **zero** separation by construction) at the arm-(b) effect size — **(L-S0-sign)** HIGH > LOW in **≥ 65%** of
    `N_S0` primes with two-sided sign-test **p < 0.05**, **AND (L-S0-median)** median(HIGH − LOW M-A Δ) over S0
    **≥ 0.01**; **otherwise (`N_S0 < 30`),** the fallback **`V = r_MA − r_W ≥ 0.20`** (M-A HIGH−LOW rank-biserial
    minus bare-weight HIGH−LOW rank-biserial, same scale-free axis). **If the applicable value-added gate fails,
    arm (b) does NOT pass regardless of (b-sign)/(b-median):** the result is reported as **NOT YET, "mechanical
    weight map, not priming-through-the-loop."** Clearing (b-sign)/(b-median) is **necessary but not
    sufficient** — the loop-work gate is conjunctive with them (§6.3). Which form applied and `N_S0`/`N_S+` are
    reported.

### Arm (c) — graded corroboration (CONTINUOUS; supporting only, never the headline)

- **Direction:** SWOW R123.Strength **positively** predicts target activation among equidistant (1-hop)
  direct targets.
- **Threshold:** **Spearman ρ > 0** (on M-A Δ; M-B reported alongside) with **p < 0.05.** This is
  **corroboration**; it can never rescue a failed categorical arm (§5.1). *(For reference only, not a bar: 2B's
  bare-engine P1 Spearman was ρ = 0.2894; arm (c) is the closed-loop analogue.)*

### Sample-size / feasibility (binding, reported per arm — SPEC-1e §5/§6.2)

- Each arm's `N_arm` is **reported separately**; a prime may contribute to arm (c) and to arm (b)-distance-1
  without populating arm (a) or arm (b)-distance-2.
- A categorical arm requires a minimum of **`N_arm` ≥ 30** within-prime pairs to be considered **powered** for
  its sign test. If **`N_arm(a)` < 30** or **`N_arm(b)` < 30**, that arm is reported as **underpowered /
  infeasible**, with the achieved N stated — **never silently dropped.** An infeasible categorical arm means
  its hole is **not** closed and the **headline cannot be earned** (NOT YET). **If BOTH categorical arms are
  infeasible**, the run falls back to **arm (c)-only** (SPEC-1e D-2 fallback) and reports the graded result as
  **NOT YET on the headline**, stating which arm(s) failed to populate at what N.

## 7.1 What would FALSIFY the claim (stated in advance — SPEC-1e §7.2)

- **Arm (a) fails:** STRONG-RELATED ≈ MATCHED-CONTROL (no separation once reachability/distance held constant;
  any of (a-sign)/(a-median-Δ)/(a-median-raw) not met) → **NOT YET** (the 2B effect was reachability, not
  priming).
- **Arm (b) fails:** HIGH ≈ LOW once distance held constant (any of (b-sign)/(b-median-Δ)/(b-median-raw) not
  met) → **the effect was topology/decay, not association** → **NOT YET**, reported explicitly as such.
- **Arm (b) "passes" but the loop adds no HIGH/LOW value over the bare-weight map (the §6.2 value-added gate
  fails — no S0 separation at the arm-(b) effect size, or `V < 0.20` in the fallback):** → **NOT YET**,
  *"mechanical weight map, not priming-through-the-loop"* (§6.3). A separation the bare-weight map already
  produces, on the same HIGH/LOW contrast, does not earn the headline.
- **Arm (a)'s control arm is all-zero / near-zero again** ((a-control-nonzero) not met) → the reachability
  confound is **not** broken → inconclusive on the crux, reported as such (not a pass).
- **A categorical arm is underpowered/infeasible** (`N_arm` < 30) → that arm's hole is **not** closed →
  headline **NOT YET**; if both categorical arms are infeasible, fall back to arm (c)-only and report the
  graded result as NOT YET on the headline.
- **Direction reverses in any categorical arm** (weak/unrelated/LOW more active) → that arm falsified.
- **Achieved per-arm hop-distance distributions are not equalized** (arm a, (a-distance-equalized) not met) or
  **distance is not genuinely constant across bands** (arm b, (b-distance-constant) not met) → the distance
  confound stands → **NOT YET.**
- **M-A and M-B disagree in direction on a categorical arm** (the closed-loop Δ says one direction, the bare
  spread the other) → that arm is **not** a clean pass; reported as inconsistent, **NOT YET** on that arm.
- **Determinism breaks across hash seeds** (the M-A/M-B numbers, or the selected pair lists, are not
  byte-identical across `PYTHONHASHSEED` in fresh processes) → run **VOID** (instrument failure), not a result.

## 7.2 THE VERDICT RULE (no post-hoc loosening — CONJUNCTIVE)

> The headline genuine-priming claim is **CLAIM EARNED (for this one effect)** if and only if the pre-stated
> direction holds at the pre-stated effect size (§7) on **BOTH arm (a) AND arm (b)** — with **arm (b) also
> clearing the §6.2 HIGH/LOW value-added-over-bare-weight gate** (HIGH > LOW on the weight-blind stratum S0 at
> the arm-(b) effect size when `N_S0 ≥ 30`, else `V = r_MA − r_W ≥ 0.20`) — with **arm (c) in the same
> direction** as corroboration, and **determinism intact across hash seeds.**
>
> **Passing arm (c) alone, or only one categorical arm, is NOT YET.** If it is **arm (b)** that fails, the NOT
> YET is reported as *"explained by topology/decay, not association."* If arm (b) "passes" only as a weight
> readback (the §6.2 value-added gate fails — no HIGH/LOW separation beyond the bare-weight map's own), the NOT
> YET is reported as *"mechanical weight map, not priming-through-the-loop."*
> Otherwise **NOT YET**, reported honestly; **1c/1d stay `BUILT · UNVALIDATED`.**
>
> (The **R1 first-response robustness re-run** is run **only if** the headline is EARNED on R123.)

A **null is a real, citable result** — including a single-arm null. If the separation vanishes once
reachability is controlled (arm a) **or** once distance is held constant (arm b — *topology/decay, not
association*), or the loop is shown to do no real work (the §6 gate), that is reported honestly and is
publishable as *"a pure spreading-activation substrate over human association norms does not, by itself,
reproduce semantic priming beyond graph reachability/decay."*

## 9. Selection procedure — BLIND, seeded, frozen NOW (SPEC-1e §6.2; pair-list HASHES deferred to §10)

### 9.1 Frozen selection parameters (fixed in advance — project-authored STRUCTURAL knobs only)

- **Relatedness/edge signal:** **R123.Strength** (the only association signal; same for substrate and bands).
- **Edge-weight map (fixed, repo-wide):** `traverse_count = min(10, 1 + floor(R123.Strength · 100))`
  (`swow.traverse_count_for_strength`). Substrate built once on `Clock(simulated=True)`, library default
  activation knobs (energy 1.0, decay_per_hop 0.65, floor 0.02, hop_budget 6).
- **Selection seed:** **`SELECTION_SEED = 20260621`** (fixed; the §10 module is fully deterministic given this
  seed, with sorted candidate lists and a seeded shuffle — the `select_pairs` discipline). *(Distinct from 2B's
  20250531 and EXP-1c's 20250620 so the three pair sets are independent draws.)*
- **`MIN_OUTDEG = 4`** (a prime must have SWOW out-degree ≥ 4 to be a candidate; sorted candidate list, seeded
  shuffle).
- **Horizon hop-cap for "reachable":** **`HORIZON_HOPS = 3`** (a node is "in-horizon / reachable, non-zero" if
  reachable within 3 hops — justified by the measured engine horizon dying ~4 hops and the SWOW reachability
  ≤3h 47.3% / ≤4h 98.5%; capping at 3 keeps arm (a)'s control genuinely *in*-horizon and non-zero, the whole
  point of closing 2B hole a).
- **Strength bands (from the empirical R123.Strength distribution; mean 0.0123, median 0.01 — heavy right
  skew):**
  - **HIGH / STRONG band:** a cue→response direct edge whose R123.Strength is in the **top decile (≥ 90th
    percentile)** of *that prime's own* direct-edge strengths **and** > 0. *(Per-prime percentile, so each
    prime contributes its own genuinely-strong associate, not a globally-thresholded one — this avoids
    high-out-degree primes dominating the HIGH band and keeps the within-prime contrast clean.)*
  - **LOW / WEAK band:** a cue→response direct edge whose R123.Strength is in the **10th–25th percentile** of
    that prime's own direct-edge strengths **and** > 0 (a **low non-zero** band — never zero, so the LOW target
    is still a real direct response, making arm (b) a strength contrast, not a reachability contrast).
  - Both bands **> 0** by construction. The same HIGH/LOW band definitions serve arm (b)'s categorical contrast
    and the high/low ends of arm (c)'s graded range.
- **Bottleneck definition (for the optional arm-(b) distance-2 sub-arm only):** the strength of a 2-hop
  P→M→T path is the **minimum R123.Strength along the path** = `min(R123.Strength(P→M), R123.Strength(M→T))`
  (the weakest link; the standard bottleneck definition, fixed in advance so a target is not rewarded merely
  for having many weak paths). HIGH-2hop vs LOW-2hop binned by this bottleneck strength.

### 9.2 Per-arm target construction (per prime P; SPEC-1e §6.2)

- **Arm (a) — reachability control, distance-matched:**
  - **STRONG-RELATED target:** a direct response of P in the **HIGH band** (1 hop, non-zero).
  - **MATCHED-UNRELATED-BUT-REACHABLE target:** a node reachable from P **within `HORIZON_HOPS = 3` hops** with
    **no direct P→target SWOW edge** (so its association strength is absent), drawn from the sorted in-horizon
    pool; **its hop-distance recorded** so achieved per-arm distance distributions can be shown equalized.
    Because a 1-hop neighbor is necessarily a direct response (hence not "unrelated"), this control sits at **2
    hops**; the achieved distance match is **reported, not assumed** ((a-distance-equalized), §7).
- **Arm (b) — distance/decay control, strength-varied at EQUAL distance:**
  - **Distance-1 (PRIMARY):** **HIGH-direct** = a direct response of P in the HIGH band; **LOW-direct** = a
    direct response of P in the LOW band. **Both 1 hop, both reachable, both non-zero**, differing **only** in
    R123.Strength band.
  - **Distance-2 (OPTIONAL sub-arm, reported separately):** **HIGH-2hop** and **LOW-2hop**, each reachable at
    **exactly 2 hops** via a real P→M→T path and *not* a direct response of P, binned by the bottleneck
    path-strength (§9.1). **Included only if both bands populate for enough primes; otherwise DROPPED and
    reported** — the distance-1 contrast alone then carries arm (b).
- **Arm (c) — graded corroboration, equidistant:** the **full set of P's direct (1-hop) responses with their
  R123.Strength values** (no banding; the whole strength range), for the Spearman slope.
- **(exploratory MEDIATED, retained labeled):** a node at exactly 2 hops, not a direct response of P (as in
  2B).

### 9.3 Keep / report rules (SPEC-1e §6.2)

- **Keep a prime for an arm only if it yields that arm's required targets.** Report the actual **`N_arm` per

  even if it cannot populate arm (a) or arm (b)-distance-2; per-arm N is tracked **separately** so an
  underpowered arm is **visible, never hidden** inside a pooled count.

- **Persist the selected pair lists verbatim**, **one file per arm**, to `experiments/results/`

- (d) **The strength→weight residual is confronted, not hidden (the crux).** Because the substrate edge weight
  AND the band signal both derive from R123.Strength, arm (b) is partly mechanical by construction (§6.1). The

  *"Nous understands / knows what words mean"* (§1.1). This framing is fixed here and stated in the final

## 12. Prior art (the phenomenon and the control logic — NOT evidence Nous reproduces cognition; SPEC-1e §8)

- **Meyer & Schvaneveldt (1971)**, *J. Exp. Psychol.* 90(2), 227–234 — foundational semantic priming (DOCTOR
  classified faster after NURSE than after BUTTER). *Establishes the human direction* (related faster than
  unrelated). *Nous tests* the analogous activation-magnitude direction on SWOW, controlled for
  reachability/distance — **not** reaction time, **not** the lexical-decision task.
- **Neely (1991)** (and Neely 1977), in *Basic Processes in Reading* — distinguishes **automatic** (fast,
  association-driven) from **strategic** (expectancy/retrospective) priming. *Nous tests* only the analogue of
  the **automatic/prospective spreading-activation** component; it models **none** of the strategic machinery.
- **Collins & Loftus (1975)**, *Psychol. Rev.* 82, 407–428 — the spreading-activation account. *The
  theoretical lineage* the engine is inspired by; cited as engineering lineage, **not** proof Nous instantiates
  the theory.
- **Balota & Lorch (1986)**, *J. Exp. Psychol. LMC* 12(3), 336–345 — mediated priming appears in **naming** but
  **not lexical decision** → the mediated effect is task-dependent; the literature reason 1e **demotes**
  mediated to exploratory (§8 above / SPEC-1e §3.3).
- **De Deyne, Navarro, Perfors, Brysbaert & Storms (2019)**, *Behavior Research Methods* 51(3), 987–1006.
  https://doi.org/10.3758/s13428-018-1115-7 — the external human data (SWOW-EN). R1 = first-response networks;
  R123 = all-responses; strength = conditional probability of response given cue. *The external yardstick*, not
  authored by the project.

**The honest separation, stated once:** the literature establishes that *humans* show automatic semantic
priming and what its direction is. 1e tests only whether *Nous's activation, over a SWOW-weighted graph, moves
in that direction when reachability and distance are controlled and the loop is shown to do real work.* A match
is *"the direction is reproduced on external data,"* **never** *"Nous primes the way a person does,"* and
**never** *"Nous understands."*

## Revision Note — 2026-06-21 (PRE-DATA tightening of the loop-work gate)

**What changed, and when in the freeze sequence.** This is a **single, surgical, strictly pre-data** revision to
the §6.2 / §6.3 **loop-is-doing-real-work** gate and arm (b)'s `(b-loop-does-work)` condition in §7. It was made
**before any graded number of any kind existed** — no `think()` was run on any selected pair, no activation
value, no D̄, no separation, no margin, and no per-arm number was computed. The §10 reserved pair-list hash slots
are **still reserved** (un-filled); the builder's §9 instrument and §10 selector are still unwritten. Revising the
frozen pre-registration at this point is legitimate precisely **because no result exists to tamper with**, and
this note records the change transparently so the freeze history (original → revision) is visible in git.

**The original freeze.** The original pre-registration was frozen in commit **`321ad80`**
(*"docs(1e): FREEZE pre-registration EXP-1e-priming — three-arm conjunctive priming, BEFORE any graded number"*).
This revision is committed on top of it as a **separate commit**, so the diff is the audit record.

**Why it changed (the population-mismatch loophole).** The original `(b-loop-does-work)` gate was
**D̄ = median over ALL of a prime's direct targets of (1 − Spearman(raw-edge-weight ranking, M-A broadcast-Δ
ranking))**, with **D̄ ≥ 0.20** required. But arm (b)'s verdict rides **only** on the **HIGH-vs-LOW extreme
bands** — a *different population* from "all of a prime's direct targets." So the loop could have cleared
**D̄ ≥ 0.20 by reordering mid-range targets** while the **HIGH-vs-LOW gap arm (b) is actually judged on** passed
through as a coarse-ladder mechanical readback. The guard and the thing it guarded were **not measured on the
same contrast**, so the gate did not protect the exact axis it existed to protect (SPEC-1e §3.2 line 87 — the
strength→weight residual).

**What the gate is now (the fix).** The binding `(b-loop-does-work)` condition is re-pinned to the **same HIGH/LOW
contrast** arm (b) is judged on, as a **value-added-over-bare-weight** requirement (§6.2): the closed loop's M-A
HIGH-vs-LOW separation must **exceed the bare raw-edge-weight map's own HIGH-vs-LOW prediction** by a
pre-registered margin. Operationalized via the **weight-blind stratum S0** (`W_gap = W(P,T_H) − W(P,T_L) = 0`,
where the saturating integer ladder collapses HIGH and LOW to the same weight, so the bare-weight baseline
predicts **exactly zero** separation by construction): when `N_S0 ≥ 30`, the loop must produce HIGH > LOW on S0 at
the arm-(b) effect size (≥ 65% within-prime, p < 0.05, and ≥ 0.01 Δ-units median) — value the weight map cannot
produce; when `N_S0 < 30`, the fallback is `V = r_MA − r_W ≥ 0.20` on the scale-free rank-biserial axis (the
common currency). **D̄ is retained as a reported SECONDARY diagnostic only** (§6.2.1), no longer the binding gate.

**What did NOT change (byte-stable in spirit).** The **three arms** (a)/(b)/(c), the **conjunctive (a) ∧ (b)
verdict rule**, **all per-arm effect-size thresholds** (the §7 (a-*)/(b-sign)/(b-median-*)/(c) numbers), the
**SWOW-derived blind selection procedure** (§9: bands, distances, bottleneck, seed `20260621`, `MIN_OUTDEG = 4`,
`HORIZON_HOPS = 3`, blinding), the **deferred per-arm pair-list hash freeze** (§10), and the **honest framing**
(§1.1, §11, §12) are all **unchanged.** The honest verdict is identical in spirit: a HIGH>LOW that does **not**
exceed the bare-weight baseline by the margin is **not** genuine priming-through-the-loop → an honest **NOT YET,
*"mechanical weight map, not priming-through-the-loop."*** The gate was **tightened, not loosened.**
